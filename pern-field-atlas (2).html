        Horizon Honesty — what the site tells you

        • Day 1 — the original benchmark evaluated a 1-day blend; this prototype does not connect to it (coverage 0.921, within ±5pt). It is labeled FORECAST with its interval.
        • Day 7 — LightGBM quantile ensemble; coverage 0.878 < approximately 0.954 nominal → flagged miscalibrated; no advantage over climatology demonstrated.
        • Day 30 — anomaly rung (NWP is ≤16-day by design); coverage 0.734 vs approximately 0.954 nominal → climatology-anchored outlook, labeled as such on screen. Nothing beyond day 1 is silently drawn.
        The forecast service is not connected in this prototype; no operational forecast is available. This benchmark supports one-day grid-temperature forecasting versus persistence and climatology — not rotation outcomes, yields or health.

        Prototype limitations / ما لم يُنفّذ بعد

        هذه نسخة تجريبية ببيانات افتراضية وليست توصية زراعية. النتائج المعروضة لا تمثل بيانات ناسا الحقيقية أو قراءات حساسات.

        Not implemented: live NASA datasets, real orbit passes, real physical sensors, connected PERN forecasts, checked payload lineage/reproduction, offline service worker or sensor-write queue, complete Arabic translation, complete provenance on all numbers, full FAO-56 Penman–Monteith, field-validated rotations, security/backend, and acceptance/performance/accessibility tests. The model uses Hargreaves ET0 with unverified assumptions; crop schedules can overlap and water-balance conservation is not verified. Uncertainty examples are placeholders, not calculated intervals. Do not use rankings, alerts or irrigation amounts in actual farming.
